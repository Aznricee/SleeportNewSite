DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Processing, usage, and safeguarding of personal data belonging to web visitors and guests of Sleeport Hotel.

1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection of personal data provided by visitors to the Sleeport Hotel website and guests using our accommodation services.

1.2. Data processing is executed in full compliance with the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" No. 2297-VI and international security conventions.

1.3. By utilizing our website, sending inquiries, or booking rooms, the user provides voluntary consent to the processing of their information under the terms of this Policy.

2. Categories of Collected Data

2.1. The hotel may collect and process:

  • Full name of the guest;
  • Telephone numbers and email addresses;
  • Government identity document details (necessary for registration during check-in as mandated by Ukrainian hospitality regulations);
  • Order parameters (dates of stay, room category, special notes);
  • Payment information required for billing, accounting, and fiscal processing;
  • Technical data: cookies, IP addresses, browser specifications, and session duration.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

3.1. Collected information is utilized exclusively to:

  • Execute, confirm, and manage room reservations;
  • Register arriving guests in accordance with hotel operational regulations;
  • Address client requests, provide customer care, and arrange logistics;
  • Satisfy statutory accounting, tax, and reporting obligations;
  • Enhance website performance, user convenience, and service quality.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing

4.1. The hotel maintains technical and organizational measures preventing unauthorized access, leakage, or loss of private data.

4.2. Data is retained solely for the timeframe needed to fulfill operational obligations or statutory regulatory requirements.

4.3. Personal information is never transferred to third parties, excluding partner banking acquiring institutions for transaction settlement or authorized legal authorities under Ukrainian law.

5. Cookies Usage

5.1. The website uses cookies to ensure correct rendering, store regional language preferences, and analyze aggregated visit metrics.

5.2. Users may configure or block cookie storage at any time via web browser settings.

6. Rights of the Data Subject

6.1. Under Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection", users have the right to:

  • Know the location of storage, source of collection, and purpose of processing;
  • Access details regarding conditions of access granted to their data;
  • Demand correction or removal of their personal data if inaccurate or processed unlawfully;
  • Withdraw consent to data processing (insofar as it does not conflict with statutory tax records).
