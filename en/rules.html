LEGAL TERMS

Accommodation & Booking Rules

Terms of service, check-in procedures, code of conduct, and financial settlements at Sleeport Hotel.

1. General Provisions

1.1. These Rules are formulated in compliance with the Civil Code of Ukraine, the Law of Ukraine "On Tourism", the Hotel Service Regulations, and Consumer Protection legislation.

1.2. The hotel provides 24/7 service for arriving and departing guests.

1.3. Check-in registration requires presenting a valid identification document (domestic passport, international passport, driver's license, or official digital ID via the Diia application).

1.4. Hotel administration reserves the right to refuse service or terminate an ongoing accommodation contract (regardless of prepaid reservations) for individuals who:

  • Display noticeable signs of alcoholic, narcotic, or chemical intoxication;
  • Engage in aggressive, abusive, or disorderly conduct toward staff or fellow guests;
  • Refuse to submit legal identification or decline compliance with fire safety and public order rules.

2. Check-In Hours and Settlement Terms

2.1. Standard check-in and check-out schedule:

  • Check-in: from 14:00 local time.
  • Check-out: until 12:00 local time.

2.2. Early check-in (prior to 14:00) or late check-out (past 12:00) is an additional service granted subject to room availability, billed at established rates upon approval by reception.

2.3. Settlement is conducted in Ukrainian Hryvnia (UAH): cash, payment cards via POS terminal, or bank wire invoice transfers for corporate entities.

2.4. Local tourist tax may apply in accordance with Tax legislation of Ukraine, unless documented exemption is provided upon registration.

3. Booking and Cancellation Policy

3.1. A reservation is deemed guaranteed upon confirmation by reception and prepayment receipt (if required by applicable tariffs or group bookings of 3+ rooms).

3.2. Non-guaranteed bookings remain valid until 18:00 on the day of arrival, after which rooms may be released for general booking if the guest cannot be contacted.

3.3. Free cancellation of guaranteed bookings is accepted up to 24 hours prior to check-in day. In case of late cancellation or no-show, the hotel reserves the right to retain the first night's fee as a penalty.

4. Stay Regulations & Guest Conduct

4.1. To protect safety and collective comfort, the following is strictly prohibited:

  • Smoking tobacco products, vapes, e-cigarettes, and hookahs inside rooms, corridors, and lounges (under Article 13 of Law of Ukraine № 2899-IV). Violations incur professional cleaning fees.
  • Breaching quiet hours and disturbing other guests between 22:00 and 08:00.
  • Hosting loud parties or mass entertainment events on hotel premises.
  • Storing hazardous, flammable, explosive, chemical, or radioactive substances, as well as firearms without regulatory permits.
  • Consuming alcohol in corridors and common stairwells, or possessing illicit substances on hotel territory.
  • Transferring room keys to unregistered third parties or accommodating unregistered guests overnight.
  • Operating high-power heating and cooking appliances not included in standard room equipment.

4.2. Stays with domestic pets are permitted strictly upon prior consent from administration under adherence to sanitary standards.

5. Liability of Parties

5.1. In the event of damage or loss of hotel property, the guest agrees to reimburse the damages in full according to current hotel replacement value tariffs.

5.2. Valuable items, cash, and documents should be secured inside in-room electronic safes or handed over to the front desk safe.

5.3. For systematic or severe violations of safety regulations and quiet hours, administration maintains the authority to cancel accommodation and evict violators without compensation for the ongoing day.

For questions regarding reservations, corporate bookings, or special accommodation requirements, please contact reception at +380 96 375 95 30 or visit the front desk.
