About Us

A space where it is easy to work, unwind, and explore the rhythm of the city.

Sleeport is a boutique hotel located in the business hub of Dnipro, designed for travelers who appreciate convenience, peaceful surroundings, and thoughtful attention to detail. We ensure every stay is seamless regardless of your travel purpose.

YOUR COMFORT

A place where you can feel at ease

After an active day in the city, returning to a peaceful space to recharge is essential. We have designed our rooms to harmonize functional utility and cozy atmosphere, providing convenience for short overnight stays as well as prolonged visits.

Attentive front desk service and readiness to assist with any guest inquiries define the hospitality experience at Sleeport.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Scandinavian simplicity and functional living

At the core of Sleeport's design lies the Scandinavian aesthetic, which prioritizes practicality, ergonomics, natural lighting, and clean geometric forms.

Warm accent palettes blend seamlessly with natural textures, delivering modern interiors free of unnecessary clutter. Every element is curated to complement your well-being and peace of mind.

STRATEGIC LOCATION

Dnipro — for business and exploration

The hotel is situated at 5 Teatralna Street, Dnipro. Positioned near central transport links, it allows guests to navigate meetings, downtown offices, and city landmarks with ease.

Convenient road access makes our location ideal both for visitors traveling by car and those utilizing public transit.
